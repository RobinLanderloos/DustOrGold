# Verdicts depend on who can act on them

A "disenchant" verdict is only useful if someone can disenchant the item. A character with Enchanting gets the full verdict on every eligible item, soulbound gear included. A character without it gets a verdict only for items it can still mail to an enchanter, and only while the account-wide "I have an enchanter alt" setting is on (it defaults to on). A soulbound item on a character without Enchanting gets no verdict, in the tooltip or the list, because the player cannot act on it from that character.

A soulbound item also cannot be listed on the auction house, so its own auction price is never shown and it never goes into the list's auction group, not even on an enchanter.

## Considered Options

- **Show verdicts only on enchanters.** Rejected: mailing bind-on-equip greens to an enchanter alt is a common way to play.
- **Show verdicts on soulbound items for non-enchanters too**, since another player's enchanter can disenchant them through the trade window's "will not be traded" slot. Tried briefly on 2026-11-14 and reverted the same day: the player found soulbound items cluttering the list on characters that cannot do anything with them.
