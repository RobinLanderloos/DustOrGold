# Advice and a disenchant button, never automatic selling

The list window groups bag items by verdict with totals and offers a click-per-item disenchant button for enchanters. It has no button that sells items to a vendor. Selling is easy to automate and hard to undo: one wrong verdict from an unverified table, or one item the player meant to keep, and the item is gone once the buyback list rolls over, which matters even more in Forever's Hardcore mode. Disenchanting stays one deliberate click per item through a plain secure spell button, usable only out of combat.

A **keep list** exempts marked items from verdicts entirely, so gear saved for an alt or a later level does not sit in the "vendor" group.

## Consequences

- A "sell vendor items" button with a confirmation step can be reconsidered once the results log (ADR 0001) has confirmed the table. Until then its absence is intentional and should not be "fixed".
- Bag slot markers were also left out: the player's bag addon draws its own buttons and offers no supported way to add markers.
